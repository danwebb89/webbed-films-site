Daniel Webb

Editor · Writer · Director

I'm a film and video editor based in Gravesend, Kent. Close enough to London to make the IMAX. Close enough to the countryside to walk off a bad cut with a film score in my ears, always a few notches too loud.

I've been obsessed with films my entire life. Not casually obsessed. The kind where I'd watch something six or seven times: once for the story, then again and again to figure out why it worked. Why that cut. Why that silence. I was making films at eight years old with a Lego webcam. By the time I was a teenager I was spending my pocket money on a TV guide and a stack of VHS tapes from Argos, recording films day and night.

Editing has never just been about cutting. It's about colour and grade. Sound design. Music used in exactly the right way. A film isn't finished when the timeline is locked. It's finished when everything is working at once.

My work spans documentary, short film, feature film and branded content. It's picked up a few Cannes Dolphins, LENs awards and EVCOMs. The better reward is the moment you hand a cut back and the director sees what the film actually is for the first time. Sometimes that's a good moment. Sometimes it isn't. And occasionally — the best feeling in this entire job — someone watches something I've cut and only realises an hour in that they haven't looked away.

Avid. Premiere. DaVinci Resolve. Best tool for the job, always. No AI doing the cutting. Just ears, eyes and instinct.

I'm not a pair of hands waiting to be told what to do. I work closely with directors and producers to find the hidden shape of the story. The structure that was always there, waiting. Hundreds of hours of footage, and somewhere inside it, a film.

Webbed Films is where all of it lives — the editorial work, the short films I write and direct, and everything in between. If you want to see what that looks like, it's all here.

When I'm working and often when I'm not, you'll find me in my edit suite. Built from the ground up as a place to make films and watch them. Dark room, 5.1 surround, colour-accurate displays, a storage server that never sleeps. The kind of room where you lose track of time, and that's entirely the point. Take a look.

Oh, and one more thing — I also write and direct short films under the Webbed Films banner. Editing makes you a better director. Directing makes you a better editor. Still testing that theory.

If you want an editor invested in the whole film and not just the cut, get in touch. Or if you just want to talk films over a coffee or a beer, that works too.
